Delete Your Account

BackGammon for iOS and Android

You can delete your BackGammon account and all associated data at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

If you still have the app installed

  1. Open BackGammon and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the Account section and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm the prompt. Your account and all associated data are deleted immediately.

If you no longer have the app installed

Email us from the address you used with "Save Progress," if you set one up. Otherwise, include your in-app display name and roughly when you signed up.

Email: varshaasro@gmail.com

We'll delete your account and all associated data within 30 days of receiving your request.

What this deletes

  • Your profile: display name, level, XP, coin balance, and game statistics
  • Your streak history and monthly badges
  • Your saved email/password sign-in credential, if you set one up
  • Your authentication record

What may be retained

  • Anonymized or aggregated analytics data that can't be tied back to you
  • Records we're legally required to keep, such as for fraud prevention, kept no longer than necessary

See our Privacy Policy for more on how BackGammon handles your data.
