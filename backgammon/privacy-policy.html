Privacy Policy

BackGammon for iOS · Effective date: November 1, 2026

This policy explains what information the BackGammon app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. BackGammon is a Backgammon game with local pass-and-play and optional online multiplayer.

Information We Collect

  • Account & profile information. When you first play online, the app creates an anonymous account for you — no email, phone number, or real name is required. You choose a display name shown to other players. If you later use "Save Progress" to protect your account against losing it on reinstall, you may optionally provide an email address and password, which is linked to that same anonymous account.
  • Gameplay data. Your level, XP, coin balance, win/loss record, play streaks, daily play history, tournament results, and any cosmetic checker/dice/background themes you've unlocked or purchased with in-game coins are stored so your progress syncs across sessions.
  • Game data shared with other players. In an online match, your chosen display name and your moves are visible to the opponent you're matched or playing with, the same way they would be across a table.
  • Usage & device data. We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used — for example, which game modes are opened — which can include standard device and app information (device type, OS version, app version, and similar diagnostic data). This is not used to identify you personally.
  • Advertising data. The app shows ads through Google AdMob (banner ads, ads between matches, and an optional "watch an ad for a bonus" feature you choose to trigger). On first launch, iOS asks whether the app may track you across other apps and websites (the App Tracking Transparency prompt); if you allow it, your device's advertising identifier may be used to show more relevant ads. If you decline, you'll still see ads — just not personalized ones. See Google's advertising & AdMob policy for how that data is handled.
  • Camera. The camera is used only to scan a friend's QR code when you choose "Invite a Friend." Nothing captured by the camera is stored, saved to your photo library, or transmitted anywhere — it's read locally on your device purely to recognize the code.
  • Push notification token. If you allow notifications, a device token is stored with your account so that, in a future update, we could notify you when it's your turn. The app does not currently send any push notifications.

How We Use This Information

  • To operate local and online gameplay, including matching you with an opponent and syncing moves.
  • To save and restore your progress (level, coins, streaks, unlocked themes) across sessions and devices.
  • To show ads that help keep the app free, and to measure how well the app is working.
  • To respond if you contact us with feedback or a question.

How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your information. Data is processed by the service providers that power the app's backend and advertising, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database (game state, profile, stats), analytics, and push messaging.
  • Google AdMob — advertising.

Your Choices

  • Playing locally (pass-and-play) requires no account and sends no gameplay data anywhere.
  • You can decline the App Tracking Transparency prompt at any time from iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • You can request that we delete your account's data by contacting us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

BackGammon is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Data Retention & Security

Your account and gameplay data are retained for as long as your account is active, and are protected by Firebase's access-control rules, which restrict your data to being readable/writable only by your own signed-in account. You can request deletion at any time.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
