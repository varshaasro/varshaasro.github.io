Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change my display name?

Your display name is set the first time you sign in. There's currently no in-app way to change it after that — email us at the address above and we can help.

I lost my progress after reinstalling or switching phones — can I get it back?

By default your account is anonymous and tied to this specific install. From Profile → "Save Progress," you can link an email and password before you lose access to a device — then use "Restore Progress" with those same credentials on any install to get your level, coins, streak, and stats back. If you didn't save progress beforehand, we're unfortunately not able to recover it, since we have no way to verify which account was yours.

How do coins and tournament entry fees work? Is this gambling?

No. Coins are a virtual, in-app currency only — they're earned by playing, completing daily challenges, or watching optional rewarded ads. They cannot be purchased with real money and have no cash value; they can't be withdrawn, exchanged, or redeemed for anything outside the app. Online matches and tournaments use a small fixed coin entry fee with a fixed coin reward for winning — not a wager against other players' stakes.

Why does the app want to send me notifications or track me for ads?

Notifications (optional) would let us tell you when it's your turn in an online match. Tracking permission (also optional) lets ads be more relevant to you — declining either one doesn't limit any gameplay feature. You can change both later from Settings → the "Enable Notifications" / "Enable Ad Personalization" rows, which link straight to the relevant iOS Settings page.

The QR code scanner isn't working.

Scanning a friend's code to start a game needs camera access. If you previously denied it, open iOS Settings → BackGammon and enable Camera, or tap "Open Settings" from the scanner screen's permission message.

How do I delete my account and data?

You can delete your account from the app itself (Profile → Delete Account), or without the app installed — see Delete Your Account for both options and what gets removed.
